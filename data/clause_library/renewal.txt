TITLE: Standard Contract Renewal Term
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-TERM-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Contract renewal periods must be clearly defined, with each renewal term and applicable renewal conditions expressly stated in the agreement.

TITLE: Automatic Renewal Restriction Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-AUT-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Automatic renewal provisions must provide clear advance notice requirements and must not prevent Tata Group from declining renewal without unreasonable penalties or restrictions.

TITLE: Renewal Notice Period Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-NOT-2026-03
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Renewal and non-renewal notices should generally be provided 30 to 90 days before contract expiration, with the applicable notice period clearly specified in the agreement.

TITLE: Renewal Pricing & Commercial Review Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & FINANCIAL
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-PRI-2026-04
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Renewal pricing, rate increases, and revised commercial terms must be expressly defined or subject to Tata Group's prior approval before a renewal becomes effective.

TITLE: Renewal Performance Review Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & PERFORMANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-PER-2026-05
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Contract renewal should remain subject to satisfactory vendor performance, service levels, compliance status, unresolved material issues, and applicable business requirements.

TITLE: Renewal Compliance Review Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & COMPLIANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-CMP-2026-06
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Renewal must be subject to confirmation that the vendor continues to satisfy applicable legal, regulatory, data protection, information security, insurance, and contractual compliance requirements.

TITLE: Renewal Modification Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-MOD-2026-07
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Material changes to contractual terms during renewal must be documented through an executed amendment, renewal agreement, or other authorized written instrument.

TITLE: Non-Renewal Rights Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-NON-2026-08
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Tata Group must retain the right to decline contract renewal by providing the contractually required notice without being required to demonstrate cause or incur an unreasonable termination or non-renewal penalty.

TITLE: Renewal Continuity & Transition Standard
CATEGORY: RENEWAL & EXIT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-TRN-2026-09
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Where a contract will not be renewed, vendors must support reasonable transition, data return, knowledge transfer, and continuity requirements necessary to prevent material disruption to Tata Group operations.
