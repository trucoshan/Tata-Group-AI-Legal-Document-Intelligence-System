TITLE: Sanctions Compliance Standard
CATEGORY: TRADE & SANCTIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-TRD-SAN-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must comply with applicable economic sanctions, trade restrictions, embargoes, and restrictive measures and must not knowingly conduct prohibited transactions involving sanctioned persons, entities, territories, or activities.

CATEGORY: TRADE & SANCTIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-TRD-SCR-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must maintain appropriate screening procedures for relevant customers, suppliers, subcontractors, counterparties, and other transaction participants against applicable sanctions and restricted-party lists.

TITLE: Restricted Jurisdiction Standard
CATEGORY: TRADE & SANCTIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-TRD-JUR-2026-03
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must not route transactions, services, funds, goods, technology, or information through restricted or sanctioned jurisdictions where prohibited by applicable trade or sanctions requirements.
