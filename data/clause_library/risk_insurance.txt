TITLE: Vendor Professional Liability Insurance Standard
CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-PRO-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors providing professional, consulting, technology, advisory, or other specialized services must maintain appropriate professional liability or errors and omissions insurance throughout the contractual term.

CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-CYB-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors handling Tata Group systems, personal data, confidential information, or material technology services must maintain appropriate cyber liability insurance covering relevant data breaches, security incidents, privacy claims, and cyber risks.

JURISDICTION: India / Global

REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-AIN-2026-07

TITLE: Insurance Cancellation & Material Change Notification Standard

REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-NOT-2026-08
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must promptly notify Tata Group of cancellation, non-renewal, material reduction, lapse, or other significant change affecting required insurance coverage.

TITLE: Insurance Deductible & Self-Insured Retention Standard
CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-DED-2026-09
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendor deductibles and self-insured retentions must be commercially reasonable and proportionate to the vendor's financial capacity and risk exposure and must not materially impair the required protection.

TITLE: Insurance Claims Cooperation Standard
CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-CLM-2026-10
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must promptly notify Tata Group of material insured events affecting contractual services and must cooperate with applicable insurers, Tata Group, and authorized representatives in investigation, defense, and resolution of covered claims.

TITLE: Insurance Does Not Limit Contractual Liability Standard
CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-NOL-2026-11
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Required insurance coverage must not be treated as a substitute for or limitation of the vendor's contractual indemnification, liability, confidentiality, data protection, or other obligations unless expressly agreed.

TITLE: Insurance Survival & Post-Term Coverage Standard
CATEGORY: RISK & INSURANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-INS-SUR-2026-12
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Where the nature of the risk requires continuing protection after contract termination, vendors must maintain applicable coverage or equivalent extended reporting or tail coverage for the required period.
