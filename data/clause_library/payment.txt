TITLE: Standard Vendor Payment Terms
CATEGORY: FINANCIAL & PAYMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-TERM-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendor invoices should be payable within agreed standard payment terms, with Net 45 or Net 60 days preferred unless a different period is expressly approved.

CATEGORY: FINANCIAL & PAYMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-INV-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must submit accurate invoices containing required purchase order, contract, tax, service, and supporting documentation before the applicable payment period begins.

CATEGORY: FINANCIAL & PAYMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-VAL-2026-03
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Payments must be subject to verification and approval of delivered goods or services, contractual pricing, applicable milestones, invoice accuracy, and required internal authorization.
