TITLE: Standard Governing Law Selection
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-GOV-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Commercial agreements must clearly identify the substantive law governing the agreement, with Indian law preferred for agreements involving Tata business units in India unless an alternative governing law is expressly approved.

TITLE: Jurisdiction & Venue Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-VEN-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Agreements must clearly specify the courts or dispute forum having jurisdiction, with an appropriate Indian jurisdiction preferred for India-based Tata business arrangements unless an alternative venue is expressly approved.

TITLE: Exclusive Jurisdiction Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-EXC-2026-03
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Where court proceedings are contemplated, agreements should identify whether jurisdiction is exclusive or non-exclusive and must avoid conflicting jurisdiction provisions across the agreement.

TITLE: Conflict of Laws Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-COL-2026-04
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Governing law provisions should identify the applicable substantive law and address conflict-of-law principles where necessary to avoid uncertainty regarding interpretation and enforcement.

TITLE: Governing Law & Arbitration Consistency Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-ARB-2026-05
JURISDICTION: India / International
GUIDANCE: Governing law, arbitration seat, arbitration rules, and court jurisdiction provisions must be internally consistent and must not create conflicting mechanisms for resolving contractual disputes.

TITLE: Cross-Border Governing Law Approval Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-XBR-2026-06
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Agreements involving cross-border counterparties should use a clearly defined governing law and dispute forum appropriate to the transaction and require legal review where a foreign governing law or jurisdiction materially affects Tata Group rights.

TITLE: Mandatory Law & Regulatory Compliance Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & COMPLIANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-MND-2026-07
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Selection of governing law must not be interpreted as excluding mandatory laws, regulatory requirements, or public policy rules that apply to the parties, transaction, data, or performance location.

TITLE: Governing Law Amendment Standard
CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW & CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: POL-LAW-AMD-2026-08
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Any change to the governing law, jurisdiction, venue, or dispute forum must be documented through an authorized written amendment or contractual instrument approved by the appropriate parties.
