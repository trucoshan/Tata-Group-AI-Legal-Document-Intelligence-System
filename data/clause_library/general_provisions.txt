TITLE: Severability Standard
CATEGORY: GENERAL PROVISIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-SEV-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: If any provision of an agreement is determined to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions must remain effective to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

TITLE: Contractual Waiver Standard
CATEGORY: GENERAL PROVISIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-WAV-2026-02
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Failure or delay by either party to exercise a contractual right must not constitute a waiver of that right, and any waiver must be expressly documented in writing by an authorized representative.

TITLE: Assignment & Transfer Restriction Standard
CATEGORY: GENERAL PROVISIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-ASG-2026-03
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Vendors must not assign, transfer, delegate, or otherwise dispose of contractual rights or obligations without prior written approval from authorized Tata Group personnel, except for expressly permitted corporate reorganizations.

TITLE: Entire Agreement Standard
CATEGORY: GENERAL PROVISIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-EAG-2026-04
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: The executed agreement and expressly incorporated documents must constitute the complete understanding between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersede prior agreements, representations, and understandings relating to that subject matter.

TITLE: Contract Amendment Standard
CATEGORY: GENERAL PROVISIONS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-AMD-2026-05
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Amendments, modifications, or changes to contractual terms must be documented in writing and executed by authorized representatives of the parties unless the agreement expressly permits another documented change mechanism.

TITLE: Notices & Formal Communications Standard

JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: Contracts must specify valid methods, addresses, designated recipients, and effective dates for formal notices, including notices relating to breach, termination, renewal, disputes, and other material contractual matters.

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-FAS-2026-13

GUIDANCE: Parties must execute reasonable additional documents and take necessary actions required to give effect to contractual rights, obligations, assignments, or transactions expressly contemplated by the agreement.

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GEN-FOR-2026-14
